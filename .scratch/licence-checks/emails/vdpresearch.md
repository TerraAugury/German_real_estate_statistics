Dear Sir or Madam,

I operate [site name] ([site URL]), a free website that presents open statistics on German commercial real estate, with the data source and licence named under every chart. The site is non-commercial: it has no advertising, no paid content or services, and there is no plan to monetise it. It is run by Terra Augury Ltd.

We display series that the Deutsche Bundesbank publishes in its statistics database (dataflow BBDZ1, the system of indicators for the German commercial real estate market). The Bundesbank names vdpResearch GmbH as the original source of 25 of them:

- Commercial property price index (total, office, retail) for Germany, for the cities of supra-regional importance, and for Germany excluding those cities, each annual and quarterly (18 series). Source comment: "vdpResearch GmbH and Bundesbank calculations."
- Rent index for new lettings, offices and retail, Germany, annual and quarterly (4 series). Source comment: "vdpResearch GmbH."
- Transaction volume index (euro values) for Germany, 127 towns and cities, and 7 major cities (3 series). Source comment: Bundesbank calculations based on vdpResearch extrapolation of purchase price data from the surveyor committees.

The data is shown only as charts and figures on our pages. The site offers no download, export or API of the data, and no one can retrieve the underlying series from us.

The Bundesbank's terms for reusing ESCB statistics allow free reuse with the source quoted, but they exclude third-party data unless the originator has given permission. Your own index files can be downloaded free of charge from your website, but they carry no terms of use, so we would like your explicit agreement before we show these series publicly.

We would be grateful if you could confirm in writing:

1. That we may display these series, as published by the Bundesbank, on our public website, in charts and tables, for non-commercial purposes, and that our use as described above counts as non-commercial.
2. That the attribution below is acceptable, or the wording you prefer. We would credit vdpResearch GmbH by name as the source of the data next to every one of these series, together with the Bundesbank as the publisher, for example: "Source: vdpResearch GmbH, published by Deutsche Bundesbank (vdpResearch GmbH and Bundesbank calculations)". The credit goes to vdpResearch GmbH first, and we would link to your website if you wish.
3. Any conditions, for example on archiving past values or on the annual or quarterly averages and ratios we calculate from the series. We label those clearly as our own calculation.

We use the values and metadata exactly as the Bundesbank publishes them, we link to the Bundesbank source, and we remove a series promptly if you ask us to. Until we hear from you, these series stay off the public site.
