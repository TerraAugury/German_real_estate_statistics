## Where the meaning could shift (reasons)

1. **Terminology:** "year-on-year" became "from the year before" (German "zum Vorjahr"); "rebased" / "umbasiert" became "set to 100 in the base year" / "im Basisjahr auf 100 gesetzt" and the lead "Rebasing" / "Umbasierung" became "Base year" / "Basisjahr"; "spread" became "gap" / "Abstand" in the table; "KPI" and "reference series" gained the chart-line terms "main line" and "comparison line" / "Hauptlinie" and "Vergleichslinie" (glossary); "tint" / "Tönung" became "background colour" / "Hintergrundfarbe" (matches the tone legend); "points" became "percentage points" in the example "-0.3 percentage points" and "-2.0 percentage points" (decimals unchanged, German "-0,3" and "-2,0"); "no lag" became "no shift".
2. **Big number:** the one long sentence became a list. The five cases are unchanged. In the third and fourth cases the original gave no unit and none was added.
3. **Vacancy and employment:** split into short sentences. "Information and communication (section J)" and "finance, real estate and business services (sections K to N)" are now introduced as "official groups of economic activity (NACE sections)" / "amtliche Wirtschaftsabschnitte (WZ 2008)". The original did not say whether the groups' growth rates or their totals are added. The series itself (`office-employment`, unit "thousand persons", `complete_only`) is a total of persons, so the text says only "add the two employment groups". **Flag:** a reviewer should confirm this reading.
4. **Verdict:** "within half a standard deviation" became "within half the usual spread". "Plus or minus half a standard deviation" became "the average plus or minus half the usual spread" (German "vom Durchschnitt minus bis plus eine halbe übliche Schwankung").
5. **Table:** the Insight names in the first column are the Insight titles and are unchanged. The "Why" column was split into short sentences. "Spread" became "gap" in the vacancy row. "Raise the cost of new capital spending" became "make new investment more expensive". "Editorial judgement" became "It is an editorial judgement."
6. **Real construction costs:** "household basket" kept as in the original (consumer price index is explained in ticket 7 under the eight indicators).
