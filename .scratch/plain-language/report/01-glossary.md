# Ticket 1: proposed glossary (plain-English column)

Pending the user's approval. The same content is written into `GLOSSARY.md`: a `_Plain_` line on each existing entry and a new section "Plain-language vocabulary" for terms the glossary does not yet define. "main line" and "comparison line" apply only where the text means a chart line in an Insight.

| English term | Plain English | German |
|---|---|---|
| Real estate KPI (the category) | Keep "KPI" on the KPI pages and explain it once per page: a key figure for the property market itself. On an Insight chart it is the "main line". | Immobilienkennzahl; short form Kennzahl; chart line: Hauptlinie |
| Economic indicator | A figure from the wider economy, such as interest rates or inflation. | Wirtschaftsindikator |
| Link | How two series moved together over time. The word "Link" stays; the text says "moved together", never "caused". | Verknüpfung; the relationship is the Gleichlauf |
| Insight | A chart that puts two or more series on one axis. The word "Insight" stays. | Einblick |
| Headline measure | The "big number". The line it is compared with is the "comparison line" (the reference series). | große Zahl; Vergleichslinie (existing German text: Vergleichsreihe) |
| Verdict | The tag that says whether the latest value is higher than, lower than or in line with the average. | Einordnung |
| Tone | The green or pink shading: our own reading of better or worse for an investor in office property. | Bewertung (draft) |
| Lead Insight | Not shown to visitors under this name. Home calls it "Open this Insight". | — (internal) |
| Citation line | The source line under a chart. | Quellenangabe |
| Heat shade | The colour of a box in the Links table. | Farbabstufung |
| Lag | The timing shift: how many years or quarters one series is moved against the other. | Verschiebung (UI today also says Versatz: flagged) |
| Segment | Property type: office, retail, logistics. | Segment |
| City class | A group of cities that a source reports as one figure. | Städtegruppe |
| Not available | No data source exists for this combination. | Nicht verfügbar |
| Open source | A data source that is free to use and may be shown publicly. | Offene Quelle |
| Provisional | The licence to show this data publicly is not confirmed yet. | Vorläufig |
| Original source | Who first made the data, when another body publishes it. | Ursprungsquelle |
| Stale | The series may be out of date: no newer data was published in the expected time. | Nicht aktuell |
| Revised | The source changed earlier values after first publishing them. | Revidiert |
| Note | A short line under a chart about scope, short history or data quality. | Hinweis |
| Reviewed (German text) | Not shown to visitors under this name; German text without it shows [DRAFT] in preview builds. | Geprüft (internal) |
| Provider | Terra Augury Ltd, the company that runs the site (legal pages are out of scope). | Diensteanbieter |
| KPI (as a chart line in an Insight) | main line | Hauptlinie |
| Reference series (as a chart line) | comparison line | Vergleichslinie |
| Verdict wording | higher than / lower than / in line with the average | höher als / niedriger als / im Einklang mit dem Durchschnitt |
| Percentage points | percentage points: the gap between two percentages (from 4% to 5% is 1 percentage point) | Prozentpunkte |
| Index | index: a figure that sets one year to 100 so change is easy to see | Index |
| Rebased | set to 100 in the base year | im Basisjahr auf 100 gesetzt (replaces "umbasiert") |
| Real / nominal | real: after removing inflation; nominal: not adjusted for inflation | real (inflationsbereinigt) / nominal |
| Standard deviation | usual spread; the technical term (standard deviation) in brackets once on Methodology pages | übliche Schwankung; Standardabweichung in Klammern |
| Year-on-year change | change from the year before | Veränderung zum Vorjahr |
| Spread | the gap between two figures | Abstand |
| Ratio | one figure divided by another | Verhältnis |
| Rank correlation (Spearman) | a number from −1 to +1 for how closely two series rise and fall together | Rangkorrelation (nach Spearman) |
| n | n: the number of periods compared | n |
| Consumer price index (CPI) | consumer price index (CPI): how prices households pay change; the usual measure of inflation | Verbraucherpreisindex |
| GDP | gross domestic product (GDP): the value of everything produced in Germany | Bruttoinlandsprodukt (BIP) |
| Initial yield | first-year rent as a share of the purchase price | Anfangsrendite |
| Vacancy rate | the share of office space that is empty | Leerstandsquote |
| Permits / completions | permits: approvals to build; completions: buildings finished | Baugenehmigungen / Baufertigstellungen |
| NACE sections J and K to N | official economic groups: J = information and communication; K to N = finance, real estate and business services | Wirtschaftsabschnitte J und K bis N (WZ 2008) |
| Bund yield | the interest rate on 10-year German government bonds | Rendite zehnjähriger Bundesanleihen |
| Euribor | the interest rate banks charge each other for euro loans | Euribor |
| PSPP | the ECB’s large government bond-buying programme (PSPP) | Anleihekaufprogramm der EZB für Staatsanleihen (PSPP) |
| Not shown | not shown: too little data to show this Link or Insight | nicht angezeigt |
