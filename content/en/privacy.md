## What this site does not do

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no local storage). It uses no analytics and makes no requests to third-party hosts: fonts and scripts are served from this site itself.

## Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you open a page, Cloudflare, acting as our processor, receives your IP address, the time of the request, the address requested and your browser's user agent. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure; the legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not keep these logs ourselves; Cloudflare keeps them for the period set for its service. Cloudflare processes data under a data processing agreement, and transfers outside the EU rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
